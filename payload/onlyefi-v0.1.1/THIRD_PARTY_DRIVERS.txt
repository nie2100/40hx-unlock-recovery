# Third-party reader drivers

This package contains:

- `windows/drivers/ThrottleStop.sys`
- `windows/drivers/WinRing0x64.sys`

They are third-party low-level reader/access drivers and are not authored by this project.

The v0.1.0 package preserves the exact binary pair used by the successful hardware validation. Before redistributing these binaries publicly, review and comply with the original projects' license/redistribution terms.

The project's own production logic is in `source/windows/` and `source/efi/`.
